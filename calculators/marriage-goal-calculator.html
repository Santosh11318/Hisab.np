Formula used

Future Cost = Current Budget × (1 + inflation)ⁿ; Required SIP = Future Cost ÷ SIP growth factor.

How it works

Wedding costs in Nepal typically rise with general inflation and lifestyle expectations — this inflates today's budget to the target year and reverse-calculates the monthly SIP needed.

Why it helps

  • Removes the guesswork from 'how much should I be saving for the wedding'.
  • Shows how starting a few years earlier meaningfully lowers the monthly SIP.
  • Works whether you're saving for your own or a family member's wedding.

Worked example

A Rs 15 lakh wedding budget today, needed in 8 years at 8% inflation, costs roughly Rs 27-28 lakh then — about Rs 25,000/month at a 10% return.

FAQs

For goals within 3-5 years, a more conservative mix is often safer since there's less time to recover from a market downturn.
