Formula used

Max EMI = (Income × FOIR%) − Existing EMIs; Max Loan = Max EMI × [((1+i)ⁿ−1) ÷ (i×(1+i)ⁿ)]

How it works

Banks in Nepal generally cap your total EMI obligations (FOIR — Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) at 40-60% of monthly income. This calculator uses that ratio to estimate the largest loan you could likely service.

Why it helps

  • Gives a realistic ballpark before you approach a bank.
  • Shows how existing EMIs shrink your new-loan capacity.
  • Helps you judge whether to pay off a smaller loan first.

Worked example

On a Rs 80,000 monthly income with Rs 5,000 in existing EMIs and a 50% FOIR limit, you could likely qualify for roughly Rs 34-35 lakh at 9.5% over 20 years.
