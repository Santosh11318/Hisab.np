Formula used

Target Fund = Monthly Expenses × Months of Cushion; Time to Build = Target ÷ Monthly Saving Capacity

How it works

An emergency fund is a cash cushion sized to cover a set number of months of essential expenses if income stops unexpectedly — this shows the target size and how long it takes to reach at your current saving rate.

Why it helps

  • Gives a clear, personalized target instead of a vague 'save some cash' goal.
  • Shows the realistic timeline to build it at your current savings pace.
  • A foundational step before more aggressive investing.

Worked example

With Rs 40,000 in monthly essential expenses and a 6-month cushion target, you'd need Rs 2,40,000 — reachable in about 30 months at Rs 8,000/month savings.

FAQs

3-6 months is common for stable salaried income; freelancers or business owners often aim for 6-12 months given less predictable income.

Somewhere liquid and low-risk — a savings account or short-term fixed deposit — rather than in the stock market, since you may need it on short notice.
