Formula used

Dividend Yield (%) = Annual Dividend per Share ÷ Current Share Price × 100

How it works

Dividend yield shows the annual dividend income as a percentage of the current share price — a quick way to compare the 'income' return of different dividend-paying stocks.

Why it helps

  • Useful for comparing income potential across bank, hydropower, or insurance stocks on NEPSE.
  • Simple, widely reported metric for income-focused investors.
  • Quick sanity check alongside a stock's price appreciation potential.

Worked example

A stock paying Rs 15 in annual dividends per share at a Rs 500 price has a 3% dividend yield.
