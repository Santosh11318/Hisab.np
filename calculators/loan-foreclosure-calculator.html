Formula used

Outstanding balance = P × [(1+i)ⁿ − (1+i)ᵖ] ÷ [(1+i)ⁿ − 1], where p is months already paid and n is total months.

How it works

When you close a loan before its full tenure, you owe the remaining outstanding principal plus any foreclosure or prepayment fee the bank charges — this calculator estimates both.

Why it helps

  • Tells you the exact amount to arrange before visiting the bank to foreclose.
  • Separates the loan balance from the fee so you can negotiate the fee if possible.
  • Useful when comparing foreclosure against a balance transfer.

Worked example

On a Rs 30 lakh, 20-year loan at 9.5% after 5 years (60 months) of payments, the outstanding balance is still around Rs 27-28 lakh, plus a 2% foreclosure fee on that balance.

FAQs

NRB guidelines have periodically restricted or capped prepayment charges on certain retail loans — confirm the current rule with your bank.

If you have the lumpsum idle and the loan's interest rate is high, foreclosing usually saves money — but weigh the fee and any better use for that cash.
