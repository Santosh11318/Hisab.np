Formula used

Each month: Interest = Balance × monthly rate; Principal paid = Payment − Interest; repeat until Balance reaches 0.

How it works

Instead of solving for the EMI, this calculator takes a fixed monthly payment you can afford and works out how many months it takes to fully clear the debt, along with total interest paid.

Why it helps

  • Useful when you know your budget but not your payoff timeline.
  • Shows immediately if a payment is too low to ever clear the debt.
  • Helps prioritize which of several debts to attack first.

Worked example

A Rs 3,00,000 debt at 15% interest paid off at Rs 15,000/month clears in a little under 24 months, with roughly Rs 40,000-45,000 in total interest.

FAQs

If the payment doesn't exceed the monthly interest charge, the debt will never reduce — the calculator flags this case.

Paying the highest-interest debt first (the 'avalanche' method) saves the most money; paying the smallest balance first (the 'snowball' method) can help with motivation — both are valid strategies.
