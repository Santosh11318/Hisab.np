Breakdown

Formula used

Balance after prepayment continues amortizing at the same EMI, reducing total months and total interest versus the original schedule.

How it works

Making a one-time lumpsum payment reduces your outstanding principal instantly. Since your EMI stays the same, more of each future instalment goes toward principal, shortening the loan and cutting total interest.

Why it helps

  • Shows the real rupee value of a bonus or windfall used for prepayment.
  • Quantifies exactly how many months you shave off the loan.
  • Helps decide between prepaying a loan vs. investing the lumpsum elsewhere.

Worked example

A Rs 30 lakh, 20-year loan at 9.5% with a Rs 3 lakh prepayment in year 5 can save several lakh rupees in interest and cut the tenure by 1-2 years.
