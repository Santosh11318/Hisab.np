Formula used

Future Price = Current Price × (1 + inflation)ⁿ; Down Payment = Future Price × %; Required SIP = Down Payment ÷ SIP growth factor.

How it works

Since most home buyers finance the rest with a mortgage, this focuses on saving specifically for the down payment portion — inflating today's house price to your target year, then reverse-calculating the SIP needed for just that slice.

Why it helps

  • Focuses your saving goal on the realistic down-payment amount, not the full house price.
  • Shows how property inflation affects your target over time.
  • Pairs naturally with the EMI Calculator for the remaining loan portion.

Worked example

A Rs 1.5 crore house today, bought in 10 years at 8% property inflation, would cost about Rs 3.24 crore then — a 20% down payment of roughly Rs 65 lakh needs about Rs 32,000-33,000/month at a 10% return.
