Formula used

How it works

This combines a one-time lumpsum with ongoing monthly contributions to show the total future value of a blended investment strategy.

Why it helps

  • Models the common real-world case of starting with savings and adding to it monthly.
  • Shows how much each part (lumpsum vs. monthly) contributes to the final total.
  • Useful for planning around both a bonus and ongoing salary savings.

Worked example

Rs 2 lakh invested today plus Rs 5,000/month for 10 years at 11% grows to a healthy combined corpus, with the monthly contributions typically outweighing the lumpsum's growth over long horizons.
