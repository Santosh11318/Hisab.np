Formula used

CAGR = [(Latest Revenue ÷ Starting Revenue) ^ (1 ÷ years)] − 1

How it works

This applies the CAGR formula to business revenue, smoothing multiple years of growth (or decline) into a single, comparable annual growth rate.

Why it helps

  • Lets you compare growth against competitors or industry benchmarks.
  • Useful for investor pitches and board reporting.
  • Removes noise from one unusually good or bad year.

Worked example

Revenue growing from Rs 50 lakh to Rs 80 lakh over 3 years works out to about 17% CAGR.
