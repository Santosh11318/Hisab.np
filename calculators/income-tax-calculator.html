Formula used

Tax = Σ (taxable amount in each slab × that slab's rate)

Nepal uses a progressive slab system for resident individuals under FY 2083/84: 1% up to Rs 10 lakh, 10% on the next Rs 5 lakh, 20% on the next Rs 10 lakh, 27% on the next Rs 15 lakh, and 29% above Rs 40 lakh. Only the income inside each band is taxed at that band's rate.

How it works

Enter your total annual taxable income (after allowable deductions like retirement contributions and insurance premiums). The calculator applies each slab in order, so crossing into a higher slab never increases the tax on income you already earned in a lower one.

Why it helps

  • See your exact effective tax rate, not just your top marginal rate.
  • Estimate the monthly TDS your employer should be withholding.
  • Compare the current slabs against last year's to see the effect of the 2083/84 tax reform.

Worked example

On an annual income of Rs 12,00,000, FY 2083/84 tax works out to roughly Rs 60,000 — versus about Rs 2,35,000 under the older FY 2082/83 slabs, illustrating the scale of the recent rate cut. Toggle "compare" above to see this for your own income.
