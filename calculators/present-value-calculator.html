How it works

Present value answers 'what is a future rupee amount worth today?' — because money available now can be invested and grow, a rupee received in the future is worth less than one in hand today.

Why it helps

  • Useful for comparing a future payout against a lumpsum offer today.
  • Helps evaluate whether waiting for a larger future sum is worth it.
  • A core building block for comparing any two cash flows across time.

Worked example

Rs 10,00,000 promised 10 years from now is worth only about Rs 3.86 lakh today, at a 10% discount rate.
