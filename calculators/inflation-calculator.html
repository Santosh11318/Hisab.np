Formula used

Future Cost = Current Amount × (1 + inflation rate)ⁿ

How it works

This projects what today's rupee amount will cost in the future given a steady assumed inflation rate — the same math used to inflate expenses in the Retirement Corpus Calculator.

Why it helps

  • Makes long-term inflation's effect concrete instead of abstract.
  • Useful input for any long-term financial goal planning.
  • Helps set realistic future price expectations.

Worked example

Rs 1,00,000 worth of expenses today would cost roughly Rs 1,97,000 in 10 years at 7% average inflation.
