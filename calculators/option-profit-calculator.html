Formula used

Call payoff = max(Spot − Strike, 0) − Premium; Put payoff = max(Strike − Spot, 0) − Premium; Total = payoff × Lot Size.

How it works

This is a general-purpose option payoff calculator: it shows the profit or loss of a call or put option at expiry based on where the underlying price ends up relative to the strike price.

Why it helps

  • Useful for learning how option payoffs work before trading them.
  • Shows the breakeven point implicitly (where payoff = 0).
  • Works for any market that lists call/put options.

Worked example

A call option with a Rs 500 strike, Rs 15 premium, and the stock at Rs 540 at expiry nets Rs 25 per unit — a Rs 2,500 profit on a 100-unit lot.
