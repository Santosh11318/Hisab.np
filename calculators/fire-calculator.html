Formula used

FIRE Number = Annual Expenses × (100 ÷ Safe Withdrawal Rate)

How it works

FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) targets a corpus large enough that a conservative withdrawal rate (commonly 4%) covers your annual expenses indefinitely — this calculates that number and how long it takes to reach with your current savings and monthly investment.

Why it helps

  • Turns the FIRE movement's core idea into your own personalized number.
  • Shows exactly how additional monthly investment shortens your timeline.
  • Works for full retirement or a partial 'lean FIRE' target.

Worked example

Rs 6,00,000 in annual expenses at a 4% withdrawal rate needs a Rs 1.5 crore FIRE number — investing Rs 30,000/month at 11% from a Rs 10 lakh start gets there in a bit over 20 years.
