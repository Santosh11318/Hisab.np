Formula used

Markup (%) = (Selling Price − Cost Price) ÷ Cost Price × 100

How it works

Markup measures profit as a percentage of the cost price, which is how most retailers think about pricing: 'add X% on top of what I paid.'

Why it helps

  • Matches how most small businesses actually price goods.
  • Easy to apply consistently across a product catalog.
  • Complements the Profit Margin Calculator for a full pricing picture.

Worked example

A product costing Rs 500 sold for Rs 750 has a 50% markup (even though its profit margin is only 33%).

FAQs

Because markup divides by the smaller cost price while margin divides by the larger selling price, so markup is always numerically higher for a profitable sale.

Use markup if you think in terms of 'cost plus a percentage'; use margin if you're targeting a specific share of revenue as profit.
