Breakdown

Formula used

Brokerage follows SEBON's tiered ceiling rates (0.36% down to 0.24% as transaction size grows); plus a 0.015% SEBON fee and a flat Rs 25 DP charge.

How it works

Every NEPSE buy or sell involves a tiered broker commission (bigger trades pay a lower percentage), a small regulatory SEBON fee, and a flat DP (depository) charge on sell transactions.

Why it helps

  • Shows the real, all-in cost of a trade before you place it.
  • Reveals why very small trades pay a disproportionately higher percentage.
  • Useful for estimating break-even price after all charges.

Worked example

A Rs 1,00,000 transaction falls in the 0.33% slab, costing roughly Rs 330 in brokerage plus a small SEBON fee and the Rs 25 DP charge — about Rs 370 total.

FAQs

These are SEBON-set ceiling rates; individual brokers may legally charge less, though most bill at or near the maximum.

The DP charge is typically applied per scrip on the sell side when shares move out of your demat account — confirm with your specific broker.
