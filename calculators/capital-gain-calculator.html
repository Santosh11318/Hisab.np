Formula used

CGT = max(Sale Value − Purchase Value, 0) × rate, where rate is 7.5% if held over 365 days, or 10% if held 365 days or less.

How it works

Nepal taxes capital gains on listed shares at source when you sell at a profit, deducted automatically by your broker via CDS and Clearing — the rate depends on how long you held the shares.

Why it helps

  • Shows the after-tax proceeds before you place a sell order.
  • Highlights the tax benefit of holding shares over 365 days.
  • Reflects the current FY 2083/84 rates.

Worked example

Shares bought for Rs 1,00,000 and sold for Rs 1,30,000 after 200 days (short-term) owe 10% of the Rs 30,000 gain — Rs 3,000 in tax.
