Formula used

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities; Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities

How it works

Working capital shows whether a business has enough short-term assets to cover its short-term obligations — a positive number and a current ratio above 1 generally indicate healthy short-term liquidity.

Why it helps

  • Quick health check for a business's short-term solvency.
  • Standard metric lenders check before extending credit.
  • Helps catch a liquidity squeeze before it becomes a crisis.

Worked example

Rs 25 lakh in current assets against Rs 15 lakh in current liabilities gives Rs 10 lakh working capital and a current ratio of 1.67.

FAQs

A ratio between 1.5 and 3 is often considered healthy, though it varies by industry — too high can mean idle assets, too low signals liquidity risk.

Current assets are cash, receivables, and inventory expected to convert to cash within a year; current liabilities are payables and debts due within a year.
